Color and Texture: Look for a golden yellow hue and a grainy texture, which indicates traditional bilona churning.

Consistency: Pure A2 ghee should remain semi-solid at room temperature and melt smoothly without residue.
Any irregularities can be a sign of additives or impurities.

**Tests for Checking A2 Desi Ghee Purity**
Spotting high-quality A2 Desi Ghee takes more than aroma and color, so a few simple tests are worth doing to confirm it's pure.
Start with the freezing test: put a tablespoon of ghee in the fridge for 30 to 45 minutes. Pure A2 ghee should solidify uniformly.
Then the melting test, where it should turn into a clear golden liquid at room temperature.
For the water test, stir a spoonful of ghee into room temperature water. Pure ghee stays at the bottom without dissolving.
And look for that rich, golden color and a grainy texture, which tells us it's been churned the traditional way.
These tests are how we make sure we've got genuine A2 Desi Ghee.
